### The Hypothesis Generator writes specs (#284)

Card 6 of the strategy-factory plan. The proposer may now name `signal-spec`
and write the paper's score as a formula over the feature library. That covers
prices, volumes, market cap, and filed SEC figures read from their filing date.
The Evaluator's own parser judges the formula. Identity is the formula's shape
with numbers excluded, and a spec equal to a named rule answers to that rule.
The model can therefore neither mint a new root by changing a window nor
propose momentum twice. A formula paper is now a strategy, not a
`missing-scorer` gap.

**Evaluated on 44 real items with the live model: 1 proposed, 18 gaps, 25
refused.** The one proposal had been a gap before this card. Seventeen of the 18
gaps need data the firm does not store. **The constraint is the corpus:** arXiv
q-fin rarely publishes an equity anomaly that is a formula, and the classic
anomaly literature the first spec batches came from is never ingested. The
same item came back as `top` in one run and `bottom` in another, so HG spec
proposals need reading, not trusting.
