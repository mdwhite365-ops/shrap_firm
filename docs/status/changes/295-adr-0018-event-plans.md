### ADR-0018: event plans. Prepare, don't predict (#295)

**Proposed, no code.** The firm reacts to a market that has already moved and
has no view of any date before it arrives. ADR-0018 proposes event plans:
written, expiring rules with an observable trigger, an action, a limit and an
end time, for scheduled events (Fed, CPI, elections, earnings, IPOs) and for
shocks that cannot be scheduled (war, a commodity spike), where the plan is
written in advance and keyed to a trigger.

Mike's rulings of 2026-11-11 are recorded: **the firm activates plans itself**,
the yardstick is **growth faster than holding the S&P**, and plans apply to
**all three accounts**.

**Merging it = accepting the Tier B evidence gate.** Defensive actions (scale
buys down, pause, trim) are live from the first card. Offensive ones (raise
exposure, open names, rotate) are also activated by the firm with no sign-off,
but only for a plan type whose graded shadow record shows it beat doing
nothing. That is ADR-0015's kill-asymmetry applied to plans.

Every plan expires and is health-checked, because of #291 and #293. Event types
are chosen by looking back at what has moved the firm's own names. Eight cards
are sequenced; the first four need no model and no new data.
